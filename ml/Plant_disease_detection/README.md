# 🌿 Plant Disease Detection Module

> **A module of Krishi Sahayak – AI & AR Smart Agriculture Assistant**

---

## 📖 Overview

The **Plant Disease Detection** module is one of the core AI components of the **Krishi Sahayak** project. It is designed to detect plant diseases from leaf images using **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Deep Learning, and Computer Vision**.

The model will analyze an uploaded leaf image, identify whether the plant is healthy or diseased, predict the disease with a confidence score, and provide suitable treatment and preventive recommendations to help farmers take timely action.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Detect plant diseases from leaf images using AI and Computer Vision.
- Classify healthy and diseased plant leaves.
- Predict the disease with a confidence score.
- Recommend suitable treatment and preventive measures.
- Reduce crop loss through early disease detection.
- Integrate the trained AI model with the FastAPI backend and Farmer Dashboard.
